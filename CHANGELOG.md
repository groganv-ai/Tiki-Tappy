# v0.2 — 2026-10-18

- Player mode now uses five columns: Goal, Shot, Defence, Pass and Special.
- Goal skips the already-selected scorer; Shot combines quality and outcome.
- Defence uses explicit subtype/quality choices; Special combines named contributions with aggregate counts and undo.
- Pass keeps tap / 1.5-second / 3-second quality capture. Both modes share event records and reports.

- Report now leads with the scorecard and switchable By action / Timeline views.
- Share the chosen report as readable PNG pages with scorers, assists, key actions, notes, award and photo.
- Video highlights are secondary; the full text/event log is collapsed for reference.

- Refined low-cut football boot icon with laces, sole plate and four bladed studs.
- Shared target geometry for shot buttons and highlight video icons.
- Linked opposition shots lead with our keeper’s save quality/name/glove icon in highlights; shot quality is secondary.

- Action-first Coach mode: Goal, Shot, Defence, Pass and Special.
- Per-action quality and Team Values contributions, separated from player choices.
- Linked goal/assist and opposition shot/keeper save capture.
- Detailed 26-zone pitch; legacy 12-zone events retained.
- Two-team scorers, assists, key-action summaries and timeline.
- Selectable, duration-budgeted highlights; no quality stars.
- Existing device data, match timing, reports and Player mode retained.
